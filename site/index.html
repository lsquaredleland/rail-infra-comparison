Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), from the OSM planet file of 28 Sep 2026. Station names appear once you zoom in far enough for them to fit. Rail comes from OSM track data, the same source OpenRailwayMap renders, joined to route relations for line names and colors. High-speed rail is any track tagged high-speed or with a line speed of 200 km/h or more. Yards, sidings, industrial and freight-only track are left out. Network lengths count parallel tracks once. City limits are official boundaries with open water trimmed along the OSM coastline; Zurich's figure includes its share of the lake.
